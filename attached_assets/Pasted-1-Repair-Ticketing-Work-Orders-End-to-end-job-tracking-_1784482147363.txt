1.Repair Ticketing & Work Orders:
	End-to-end job tracking from initial intake to quality assurance and customer pickup. 
Include features to log device passwords, record intake photos (to document pre-existing damage), and assign specific technicians.
IMEI & Serialized Inventory: Track individual device histories, parts used, warranty status, and vendor purchase orders. 
The system should automatically deduct replacement parts from stock when a repair is completed.
2. Repair-Focused POS: Unlike standard retail POS, it must handle 
repair deposits, diagnostic fees, split payments, and pickup balances in a single workflow.
including Real-time tracking improves inventory accuracy and reduces delays.
Serialized, refurbished, and bundled parts should be easy to manage.
Multi-location inventory visibility helps balance stock across branches.
Automatic adjustments sync inventory with active repairs.

3. Automated Customer Notifications: 
Trigger real-time SMS and email updates to customers as their repair status changes (e.g., "Device Received," "In Progress," "Ready for Pickup").,
Stores customer profiles with device history and preferences.
Sends automated status updates and appointment reminders.
Segments customers for promotions and loyalty programs.
Builds stronger trust and drives repeat business.
4.Customer Relationship Management (CRM): 
Keep complete records of past repairs, device history, and customer communication tied to every client profile.
5.Employee & Commission Management: 
Role-based access control, 
clock-in/clock-out tracking, shift scheduling, and technician commission tracking.
6.Reporting, Analytics & KPI Dashboards:
Provide real-time dashboards for performance tracking.
Show weekly and monthly trends.
Track profit margins, repair volume, and inventory turnover.
Support flexible exports for deeper financial analysis.
6.Cloud-Based Repair Shop Software: 
Access software from any device.
Update repair statuses on the go.
Use cloud backups for reliability.
Support smoother work across teams and locations.
7.Third-Party Integrations: 
Integrations with accounting and supplier tools.
SMS and email gateways for better communication.
Barcode and QR code scanner support.
Open APIs for custom workflows.
